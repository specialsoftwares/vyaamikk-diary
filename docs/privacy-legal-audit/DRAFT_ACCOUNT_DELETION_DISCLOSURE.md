## How to delete your Vyaamikk Diary account

### In the app (recommended)

1. Open **Vyaamikk Diary** and sign in.  
2. Go to **Settings**.  
3. Tap **Delete Account & Data**.  
   (You can also open **Settings → About → Delete account**.)  
4. Read the confirmation carefully and confirm.

### On the web

Visit: **https://vyaamikk.specialsoftwares.com/delete-account**

Follow the instructions on that page. If the page asks you to email support, include your registered mobile number and Vyaamikk ID (UEID) if known.

Support: **support.vyd@specialsoftwares.com**

**[EXTERNAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED: whether the live page initiates a tracked deletion request or is instructions/mailto only]**

You do **not** need to reinstall the App to start an in-App deletion when you can still sign in.

## What happens after you confirm

1. Your account enters **pending deletion** with a **15-day grace period**.  
2. During this period, access to normal App use is restricted according to product rules.  
3. Where reactivation is offered, it requires **in-App verification** (for example a verification code to your registered business email). **Replying to an email is not enough to reactivate.**  
4. After the grace period, we run final deletion steps for **App-controlled cloud database records** linked to your account.

---

## What we delete (App-controlled)

After finalisation, we delete or de-identify App-controlled cloud data such as:

- Profile fields used for the account  
- Synced business records in our cloud database subcollections  
- Related cloud indexes needed for login (subject to limited security retention below)

---

## What may remain

- **Limited security / anti-abuse records**, such as retired mobile bindings, so a deleted identity cannot be misused  
- **Data on your device** (local database, files) until you clear App storage or uninstall  
- **Exported or shared PDFs** and copies on WhatsApp, email, print, or other apps — we cannot delete those

- Records we must keep under applicable law  
- Limited operational deletion-job metadata (no business record content) — retention **[OWNER CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]**

---

## Account freezing vs deletion

Pending deletion is a **time-limited grace state**, not a permanent freeze offered instead of deletion. Finalisation is intended to permanently close the account and remove App-controlled cloud records as described above.
