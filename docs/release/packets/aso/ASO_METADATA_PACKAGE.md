## Full description (≤4000)

Vyaamikk Diary helps Indian MSME owners and staff keep daily business records on a phone: cash movements, material movement, customer credit notes, reminders, and letterhead documents as PDFs.

Goods Receipt and Inspection (GRIN) records what arrived, inspection notes, and supporting documents together when your organisation has this feature enabled. Historical basic goods-received notes remain available to read and export. Returns link to your own receipts; registering a return does not invent replacement stock.

Your records stay tied to your signed-in account. UEID is an internal business association — not government verification and not a public worker profile.
