Delete inside the app (recommended)

If you can sign in to Vyaamikk Diary on your device, use the in-app deletion flow:

  1. Open Vyaamikk Diary and sign in with your registered mobile number.
  2. Go to Settings (Info tab).
  3. Open Delete Account & Data under Account & App.
  4. Read the confirmation screen carefully, then confirm deletion.
  5. Your account enters a 15-day grace period. You may cancel during this period by signing in again if the app offers restore — otherwise data is scheduled for erasure after the grace period.

Request deletion from the web

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account (if any) or from a mailbox you can verify:

Email deletion request

Include:

  • Your registered mobile number (required to locate the account)
  • Your Vyaamikk ID (UEID) if you know it
  • A brief statement that you want account and data deletion

We may verify ownership via OTP or additional checks before processing web requests.

What data is deleted

After the grace period completes, we delete or de-identify app-controlled data associated with your account, including:

  • Account profile (name, business details, UEID binding)
  • Business records stored in our cloud sync (when enabled)
  • Drafts, master-data suggestions, and sync queue entries tied to your user ID
  • Authentication session and server-side identity records subject to our retention policy

Data on your device may remain until you uninstall the app or clear app storage; cloud deletion does not automatically wipe your phone's local database.

What we cannot delete

Exported and shared PDFs cannot be recalled.

If you generated PDFs and shared them via WhatsApp, email, print, or any channel outside Vyaamikk Diary, those copies are under your control and ours. We cannot delete files on third-party services, recipients' devices, or physical copies.

We may retain minimal records where required by law, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution, as described in our Privacy Policy.
