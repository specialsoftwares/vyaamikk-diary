Architecture

Technical Superiority

Every record is designed to become searchable business memory — not just another note.

Input → Structured Record → Secure Save → PDF/Text Share → Search/Insights
  • Stable record identity across edits and sync
  • Duplicate-save protection via idempotency patterns
  • On-device PDF generation with user-controlled sharing
